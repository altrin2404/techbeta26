
# TECHBETA26 — College Tech Symposium Website

A futuristic, dark-themed, fully responsive website for TECHBETA26 with neon accents, glowing effects, and smooth animations.

---

## 🎨 Design & Theme
- **Dark background** with neon blue/cyan/purple glowing accents
- Futuristic typography and subtle glow/pulse animations
- Fully responsive — works seamlessly on desktop and mobile
- Smooth scroll navigation between sections

---

## 📄 Pages & Sections (Single-Page Layout)

### 1. Hero Section
- Bold "TECHBETA26" branding with glowing animated text
- Event tagline, date, venue
- CTA button linking to external registration (Google Forms or similar)

### 2. About
- Brief description of the symposium and the department hosting it

### 3. Events & Schedule
- Cards for each event/competition with name, description, timing, and rules
- Clean grid layout with hover glow effects

### 4. Team / Speakers
- Photo cards for organizers, coordinators, or guest speakers
- Name, role, and optional social links

### 5. Bus Routes
- List of college bus routes with bus numbers and timings
- Clean, easy-to-read table or card layout

### 6. Sponsors & Contact
- Sponsor logo showcase
- Contact details (email, phone, social media links)
- Venue/location info

### 7. FAQs
- Accordion-style frequently asked questions section

---

## 🧭 Navigation
- Sticky top navbar with smooth-scroll links to each section
- Mobile hamburger menu
- "Register Now" CTA button always visible

---

## ⚡ Key Features
- No backend needed — purely informational site
- Placeholder content that's easy to swap with real data
- Fast-loading, lightweight, and mobile-friendly
